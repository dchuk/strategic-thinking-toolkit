Step 1 — Capture Key Purchasing Criteria

KPCs are what your stakeholder actually decides on. Aim for 6 to 8 short noun phrases — bias to outcomes, not features. ("Onboarding speed" beats "self-serve wizard".)

      Phase 03 · Rate

      Plot the Lazy-L

      Each KPC sits in a fixed column by importance — most important on the left. Drag pins up or down to mark how satisfied your stakeholder is. The dashed line is the satisfaction profile; pins in the warm-sand zone are your strategic priorities.
